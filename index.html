Our Story

a quiet corner of irbid that smells like home.

AJDA began with a simple idea — that the best conversations happen in the soft hum of an espresso machine, in a room warm enough to forget the time. We pour that feeling into every cup.

مَعَ بِيئَتِنَا الدَّافِئَة وَالهَادِئَة وَمَجْمُوعَتِنَا المُتَنَوِّعَة مِنَ المَشْرُوبَات وَالحَلْوِيَّات اللَّذِيذَة، يُمْكِنُكَ الاسْتِمْتَاع بِتَجْرِبَة لَا تُنْسَى فِي أَجْدَا كَافِيه.

The Process

from bean to cup.

01

single origin

We source from the world's best coffee regions — Brazil for body, Ethiopia for brightness, Colombia for balance.

02

small-batch roast

Roasted locally, in small batches, to keep the bean honest and the flavor full.

03

hand-pulled

Every espresso is pulled by hand. Every milk is steamed to silk. No machines decide for us.

04

served warm

From the bar to your table in under three minutes — and never lukewarm.
